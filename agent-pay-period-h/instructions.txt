INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AGENT (her setup, PR 12's end check)

You are checking a personal budget app by using it the way its one user
would, for one full pay period. You have not seen its code, and you must not
look at it.

What you may read
- budget-app-rulebook.md: how the app must treat every kind of money
  movement. It is the only authority on what each number should be. This
  app is set up for one person ("her setup"): read the section "Off in this
  setup" first. Rules listed there never apply, and nothing about them
  (a mom reserve, an old card) should be shown anywhere.
- agent-pay-period-h/script.txt: what happens at the banks each day, and
  what you decide.
- These instructions, and your own notes.
Do not open, read, search or list any other file in this folder or anywhere
else: no code, no tests, no other documents, and not the folder
agent-pay-period/ (that is another setup's). Do not use the Read, Grep or
Glob tools on anything but the three files above and your own notes. If you
need to know how something works, use the app and the rulebook.

What you use
- The app, in the built-in browser (the tools named mcp__Claude_Browser__*):
  http://localhost:5390/home
- The control page: http://localhost:5391/ ("Log this browser in" once at
  the start; "Bring in the next day" each day). Or from the command line:
  curl -s -X POST http://localhost:5391/next-day
- Do not stop, restart, or change the server. Do not use any other address
  or any other page of the control server.
- Everything is made up: no real bank, no real money.
- The browser may be set to a phone's size (375 x 812) to use the app as
  she would; that is fine either way.

Each day
1. Bring in the day (the script says what the banks show).
2. Do what "You decide" says, in the app, like a person: tap, choose, type.
   If the app asks you to confirm, say yes unless the script says otherwise.
3. Then look at the screens. Every day: Home (with each card on it),
   Buckets, Review, and the Wishlist list. On Oct 1, Oct 3, Oct 5, Oct 7,
   Oct 8, Oct 10, Oct 13 and Oct 16 also every other screen: the list of
   each bucket (Needs, Wants, Savings, Wishlist, Held for others), Payday,
   Settings and its Status page, each card's statement (tap the card on
   Home), and Trends.
4. For every number on every screen you look at, write down what the
   rulebook says it should be, worked out by you from the rulebook, the
   script, and your own running record of the buckets (keep it in your
   notes), and what the screen shows. Work out the rulebook number first,
   then look. Show your working for anything that is not obvious (a split,
   a rounding, a card's statement, what is set aside, a Wishlist item's
   share, the move from savings to checking).
5. Mark each difference clearly: DIFFERENT, with both numbers.
6. Also check, on every screen you look at, that nothing mentions a mom
   reserve, a payment for mom, or an old card, and that Wishlist items and
   cards say their state in words ("Ready", "Due"), not only by color.

Your notes and report (plain text, in plain language)
- agent-pay-period-h/notes.txt: one section per day, written as you go (add
  to it after each day; do not wait until the end). For each screen: each
  number, "rulebook X, screen Y", and same or DIFFERENT. Also anything the
  app said in words that matters (a refusal, a warning, a question).
- agent-pay-period-h/report.txt, at the end: the bottom line first (did
  every number match the rulebook?), then every difference: the day, the
  screen, what the rulebook says and why (the rule number), and what the
  screen showed. Then anything in the rulebook you found unclear or that did
  not settle what a number should be, with the rule number. Then anything
  that was hard to do on the screens.
- Write only these two files. Change nothing else.
