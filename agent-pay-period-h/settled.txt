The agent (PR 12's end check) used her setup's screens on the real server
with two made-up banks for the whole pay period, Thu Oct 1 to Fri Oct 16,
without seeing the code. Its instructions, the script it was given, its
notes and its report are in this folder. npm run pay-period-agent-her starts

through the server's desks in packages/server/test/pay-period-her.test.ts,

by hand.

every day: every bucket, safe to spend, the self-check (and its waiting on
Oct 7, when the second bank wanted a login), both paycheck splits, the
payday questions, move to savings, both cards' statements, paid and set
aside, the Wishlist fill, the 50.4 moves, and Trends' estimates. Its final
books (savings 8,228.97, needs 780.00, wants 963.55, Wishlist 644.01, held
for others 0.00) are the ones worked out by hand in pay-period-her.test.ts.

rulebook does not settle in words, each settled below by a reading he
