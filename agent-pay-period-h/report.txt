ANYTHING IN THE RULEBOOK THAT WAS UNCLEAR OR DID NOT SETTLE A NUMBER

a. 4.5 / 13.8 / 40.6, finding a place's name on a quiet account.
   "None on most of your items ... in the 90 days before" does not say
   whether the item itself counts. The app counts it (and its own day),
   so a store that is most of an account's few items loses its name:
   - Both GROCER items (97.38, the biggest place on Oct 1 to Oct 3) went
     to "Everything else".
   - Both BOOKSHOP items went there too.
   - The Oct 4 checking PHARMACY (18.35) is not in "PHARMACY" (68.75),
     although 40.6 says the same name on any account is one place.
   Read strictly ("before", not counting itself), an item with nothing
   before it would get its whole description as its name ("MADE UP
   PHARMACY"), which is a different place again. Neither reading is
   clearly meant. The totals always added up.

b. 3.1 / 3.4 / 40.1, a purchase made on payday itself, before the yes.
   The CAFE of Oct 2 (the paycheck's day) came out of wants before the
   split. It was counted in the wants leftover (243.40), and Trends puts
   it in the Sep 18 to Oct 1 bar (B97: the pay period the books counted
   it in). But by its date it is in the Oct 2 pay period, and 40.14 and
   40.15 use its date (day 1 of Oct 2 to Oct 15). The rulebook does not
   say which pay period a purchase on payday belongs to while the
   paycheck waits for a yes. The app's answer is consistent, and I took
   it as right.

c. 40.3 / 40.4 / 40.14, what "cut off by the 90 days" means once the 90
   days have moved past the history kept at connection. See difference 7.

d. 40.8 on the books' first pay period. Oct 1 showed "Day 1 of 1" (from
   setup day), while 40.7 on the same screen said "day 14". 40.3 makes
   Sep 18 to Oct 1 one pay period, but 40.11 says the books' first pay
   period counts from setup day. The even-pace number (0.00) is the same
   either way.

e. 52.2, "how much of the set-aside money is in savings". I read it as
   the smaller of the savings extra (3.8) and what is set aside, counting
   money already on its way to savings as moved. The app did the same
   every day.

f. 3.8 with a transfer to savings on its way (Oct 3). I counted the
   1,200.00 already sent as moved ("how much you still need to move").
   The app showed it as "On its way to the account" and the same to-move
   0.00 and extra 806.60.


WHAT WAS HARD TO DO ON THE SCREENS

- The split form always gives Wishlist "the rest". A two-bucket needs +
  wants split cannot be made (difference 1). It also asks "Which thing on
  your list was it?" even when Wishlist's part is 0.00.
- Typing into a sheet just after it opens sometimes doubled what I typed
  ("CAFE" became "CAFECAFE"). Screenshots lagged behind what was on the
  page, so I checked field values before saving. I fixed the rule; both
  changes are in the change log.
- Savings over time (40.5) only gives the first and last amounts in
  words. The amounts at the end of each pay period are only a line I had
  to read from its height.
- Statement lines in "By bucket" and "By paycheck" have to be tapped to
  see the items. Before setup day's items are one group in both views.
- Pages load slowly at times ("Loading ..."), so each screen needed a
  few seconds' wait.
- The card headers "Due Fri, Oct 23" (just the due date) and "Due in 7
  days" (the due state) both start with "Due". The state is clear only
  from the second.
