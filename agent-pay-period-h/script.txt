THE PAY PERIOD: WHAT HAPPENS AT THE BANKS, AND WHAT YOU DECIDE

For 16 days, Thu Oct 1 to Fri Oct 16, 2026, you are the person the budget
app is for. Everything here is made up: the banks are made-up banks, and no
real money is involved. The app is the one described in the rulebook
(budget-app-rulebook.md), set up as the rulebook's "her setup". Use it in
the browser the way a person would.

Her banks and accounts, as the app names them
- The rulebook's Capital One is "Made-up First Bank" here. It has checking
  ("Made-up Checking", with a debit card), savings ("Made-up Savings"), and
  the rulebook's Capital One card ("Made-up Everyday Card").
- The rulebook's American Express is "Made-up Second Bank" here. It has the
  rulebook's Amex card ("Made-up Second Card").
- Both banks are already connected, and the four accounts are already
  picked (1.1, 1.2). Do not connect a bank and do not log in to a bank: in
  this test there is no bank login window.

How the days work
- The app shows today's date in its own world, which starts on Thu Oct 1
  (setup day). Real time does not matter.
- The control page (its address is in your instructions) has two buttons:
  "Log this browser in" (use it once, at the start, in the browser you use
  for the app) and "Bring in the next day". Each press moves the app to 7:00
  AM of the next day and puts that day's activity at the banks; the app
  reads the banks by itself right after. You can also press it from the
  command line: curl -s -X POST http://localhost:5391/next-day
- Each day: bring in the day, then do what "You decide" says, then look at
  the screens and write your notes for that day.

What the banks show
- Amounts are what the bank shows on that account. On a card, a purchase is
  money you owe; "back" or "in" means money that came back to you.
- "Pending" is a charge the bank holds and has not posted yet. When it posts
  later, the bank shows the final amount.
- A transfer between your own accounts shows as two separate items: one
  when the money leaves an account, and one when it arrives at the other. A
  payment to a card shows as "payment to CRD ..." leaving checking, and
  "PAYMENT - THANK YOU" when it reaches the card.
- Each card's statement (its balance, minimum, due date and closing day)
  comes from its bank, when the bank sends a new one.
