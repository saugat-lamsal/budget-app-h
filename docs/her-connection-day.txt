HER CONNECTION DAY (plain steps)

Her roadmap, Phase 3. The first time her real money comes near the app:
two of her Plaid team's 10 connections get used (Capital One, then
American Express), and her hosted books start fresh for her real accounts.
Nothing here happens until he says so. Anything odd: we stop.

Her app: budget-app-h-lbwr, at https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev
Her real books: volume budget_h_real (ewr, 1 GB), backups in her own R2
bucket, folder budget-h-real.
The test bank's books stay on budget_h_data and in folder budget-h,
untouched: the way back (below).

Every fly command names --app budget-app-h-lbwr, so none can reach his
app (budget-app-x7q2). Claude can run steps 1 to 6 at his word, and never
prints a key: each is checked by its shape only.

Her logins to Capital One and American Express are typed by her, in
Plaid's window. Never by him, never by Claude. Nothing from her screens
(amounts, names, account digits) goes in the repo or in a chat; anything
odd is reported as "nnnn" with no amounts.

BEFORE SHE COMES (his, with Claude; about 20 minutes; connects nothing)

1. The switch pull request (branch her-connection-day-switch): fly.toml
   says PLAID_ENV "production" and mounts budget_h_real; litestream.yml
   copies to budget-h-real. He reviews it, CI is green, he merges it.
   From then on main is for her real books: the test bank's books can no
   longer be deployed from main (the way back is below).

2. A new bank key for the new books (the old one sealed only the test
   bank's connections), made without printing it:
        node -e "console.log(require('node:crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('base64'))"
   written to a file outside the repo, for example
   C:\Users\sauga\Documents\budget-app-h-real-BANK_TOKEN_KEY.txt, then
   into the password manager, in an entry named for her app and "real
   books". Without it a rebuilt copy cannot open her bank connections.
   Keep the old one's file too (budget-app-h-BANK_TOKEN_KEY.txt): the way
   back needs it.

3. The two secrets, staged (nothing restarts yet). A file .env.fly in her
   folder with two lines:
        PLAID_SECRET=      (from .env.prod)
        BANK_TOKEN_KEY=    (from step 2)
   UTF-8 without a byte order mark. Then, in PowerShell:
        cmd /c "fly secrets import --stage --app budget-app-h-lbwr < .env.fly"
   (Not "Get-Content .env.fly | fly secrets import": PowerShell 5.1 adds a
   byte order mark and Fly refuses the first name.) Delete .env.fly and
   .env.prod. PLAID_CLIENT_ID (the same for both of Plaid's environments),
   the push keys and the R2 keys stay as they are.
        fly secrets list --app budget-app-h-lbwr
   still shows the ten names, two of them staged.

4. The new volume, and the machine moved onto it:
        fly volumes create budget_h_real --app budget-app-h-lbwr --region ewr --size 1 --yes
        fly machine list --app budget-app-h-lbwr          (the one machine's id)
        fly machine destroy <id> --app budget-app-h-lbwr --force
        fly deploy --app budget-app-h-lbwr --ha=false --remote-only
   From main, after step 1. No deploy check this once: the new folder has
   no backup yet to check against (step 12 is the check). The app is down
   for a few minutes between destroy and deploy, so UptimeRobot may send
   an alert; that is expected. The old volume (budget_h_data) and folder
   (budget-h) are not touched. Delete them yourself after her first weeks
   (about $0.15 a month until then).

5. Checks:
        fly status --app budget-app-h-lbwr          one machine, started
        fly volumes list --app budget-app-h-lbwr    budget_h_real attached,
                                                    budget_h_data not
        fly logs --app budget-app-h-lbwr            "starting with empty books",
                                                    "Budget app: the real bank, through Plaid, hosted.",
                                                    "No bank is connected yet"
   https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev/health says ok, and /health/backups
   says ok within a few minutes. In Cloudflare, budget-h-backups now has
   files under budget-h-real/. The UptimeRobot monitors stay as they are
   (same addresses).

6. Log in on the PC, in Chrome (not the Claude app's browser pane: it
   blocks the bank's login window). Open https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev.
   The passkey from the test books will not work on the new books: tap
   "Use a one-time code", and
        fly ssh console --app budget-app-h-lbwr -C "node /app/serve/login-code.mjs"
   gives 12 digits (good once, for 15 minutes). Make a passkey (Windows
   Hello). Home says Start setup day.


WITH HER (together, in this order)

7. Capital One first, on the PC, in Chrome: the Bank page, Connect a bank.
   It asks first (1 of the 10): Yes, connect. In Plaid's window: Continue
   without phone number (or as guest), search "Capital One", and pick the
   plain "Capital One" entry. Capital One's own login opens in a window of
   its own: she types her login there, and ticks checking, savings and her
   Capital One card. Plaid's window closes; the Bank page shows one bank.
   If Plaid's window says something went wrong, or the Bank page does not
   show the bank, stop and look (the Bank page after a reload, and fly
   logs) before trying again: a connection that went through counts
   against the 10 even if the page did not show it.

8. American Express, the same way: Connect a bank, Yes, connect, the plain
   "American Express" entry, her login in Amex's own window, tick the Amex
   card. The Bank page shows two banks. The app never makes a third.

9. Which account is which: Checking, Savings, Everyday card (her Capital
   One card), Second card (her Amex card). Save, then "Yes, keep them".
   If an account is missing: Log in to that bank again (no new
   connection) and tick it. If the Bank page says Plaid is still sending
   the history, "Look again" in a few minutes: setup day and the rule
   finder wait for both banks' whole history (Capital One sends about 90
   days; American Express may send more).

10. What each bank sends, checked without her numbers: Home's "Your
    cards" shows each card with a statement balance and a due date; if a
    card says "the bank did not send its statement date", she enters that
    card's closing day in Settings (from a statement). Settings > Status
    shows both banks read. Anything missing becomes a question before the
    trial starts (her roadmap, Phase 3). Claude can look at the logs, at
    his word, and report only which fields came (no amounts, no digits).

11. Setup day, with her real balances, on the PC or her phone, her
    choice: the rule finder (keep, change or skip each suggestion), the
    transfers still on their way, her last paycheck, her split (savings,
    needs, wants, Wishlist), Start tracking. The self-check must pass
    before tracking starts (14.3).

12. The restore check on the machine:
        fly ssh console --app budget-app-h-lbwr -C "node /app/serve/restore-check.mjs"
    The last line must be: The backup works.

13. Her iPhone (hers; he reads her the code). In Safari open
    https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev, Share > Add to Home Screen > Add,
    and from now on open it from the home screen only. "Use a one-time
    code": a new code from step 6's command; Face ID makes her passkey.
    Settings > Notifications > Turn on, Allow; Send a test alert with the
    phone locked: words only on the lock screen, no amount.

14. His PC afterwards: Settings, Log out, if she would rather he not stay
    logged in to her books on it.


EVERY DEPLOY FROM NOW ON

As docs/her-setup.txt step 5 says: npm run deploy-check (it now rebuilds
her real books from budget-h-real and replays every event), deploy only on
"The backup works.", then fly deploy --app budget-app-h-lbwr --ha=false
--remote-only, and the checks of step 5.


THE WAY BACK (before setup day only)

If something is wrong before setup day: fly.toml back to sandbox and
budget_h_data, litestream.yml back to budget-h (a revert of the switch
pull request), the Sandbox secret (in her .env) and the old bank key
(budget-app-h-BANK_TOKEN_KEY.txt) as secrets, the machine destroyed and
deployed again as in step 4. The test bank's books come back as they were,
still able to read the test bank. Any connection made in steps 7 and 8
stays used (2 of the 10): Plaid never gives one back. After setup day
there is no way back: her real books are the books, and a problem gets a
test and a fix (her roadmap, Phase 4).
