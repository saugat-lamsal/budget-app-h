HER APP ONLINE: HIS STEPS, THEN HERS (plain steps)

Her roadmap, Phase 2. Plaid's test bank only: nothing here touches a real
account. About an hour. No account was made from here: each step below is
his to do (or hers, where it says so), or Claude's at his word.

Her app: budget-app-h-lbwr, at https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev
Her volume: budget_h_data (ewr, 1 GB)
Her backups: her own R2 bucket, folder budget-h
If the app name is taken in step 2, pick another (lowercase letters, digits
and dashes) and change it in fly.toml in two places (app, and APP_ORIGIN).
Use your name wherever this says budget-app-h-lbwr.

Every fly command below names the app (--app budget-app-h-lbwr), so it can
never reach his app (budget-app-x7q2) from the wrong folder.

Costs: about $3.84 a month more on his Fly account (one small machine and a
1 GB volume, Fly's prices of Oct 2026). R2 and UptimeRobot stay free at
this size.


WHEN

- Steps 0 to 4 can be done any time.
- The first deploy (step 5) can come any time after PR 2 is merged. It is
  harmless early: the test bank only, and on connection day her real books
  get a new volume and backup folder anyway. Until PR 6, the test bank
  connects one made-up bank by itself on the first start; the rehearsal
  with two made-up banks waits for PR 6.
- Her real banks: only on connection day (her roadmap, Phase 3), after
  PR 12.


0. A FOLDER OF ITS OWN FOR HER REPO, ON THIS PC

   Her repo was first cloned into a folder the Claude app made for one
   session, which goes away with that session. Give it a lasting home
   next to his, in PowerShell:
        cd C:\Users\sauga\dev
        git clone https://github.com/saugat-lamsal/budget-app-h
        cd budget-app-h
        git remote add upstream https://github.com/saugat-lamsal/budget-app
        git remote set-url --push upstream DISABLED
        git config merge.ours.driver true
        npm ci
   Check: git remote -v shows upstream's push as DISABLED. Every command
   below runs in this folder (C:\Users\sauga\dev\budget-app-h), never in
   his (C:\Users\sauga\dev\budget-app).

1. HER PLAID TEAM'S TEST KEYS (her team is already made)
   a. In Plaid's dashboard, switch to her team (not his).
   b. Check it says Trial, 0 of 10 connections.
   c. Developers > Keys: copy the client_id and the Sandbox secret. Not the
      Production secret: that one waits for connection day.
   d. Never submit a Production application for her team either (it is
      one-way and ends the Trial).

2. HER FLY APP AND ITS VOLUME (on his Fly account; not deployed yet)
        fly apps create budget-app-h-lbwr
        fly volumes create budget_h_data --app budget-app-h-lbwr --region ewr --size 1 --yes
   One volume, on purpose, as on his: the R2 backups cover it.

3. HER OWN R2 BUCKET (on his Cloudflare account)
   a. R2 Object Storage > Create bucket: name budget-h-backups, location
      Automatic.
   b. R2 > Manage API tokens > Create API token:
        Permissions: Object Read & Write
        Specify bucket: Apply to specific buckets only > budget-h-backups
        TTL: Forever
      A token of its own, for her bucket only: his token cannot read her
      books, and hers cannot read his. Copy, before closing the page (shown
      once): the Access Key ID, the Secret Access Key, and the S3 endpoint
      (https://<account id>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com, without the bucket
      name after it).

4. HER SECRETS (new ones: never his)
   a. Two new keys, in her folder:
        node -e "console.log(require('node:crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('base64'))"
      (her BANK_TOKEN_KEY), and
        npm run push-keys
      (her VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY and VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY).
   b. In her folder make a file named .env.fly (git never takes it:
      .gitignore has .env.*) with these lines, filled in:
        PLAID_CLIENT_ID=      (her team's, from 1c)
        PLAID_SECRET=         (her team's Sandbox secret, from 1c)
        BANK_TOKEN_KEY=       (from 4a)
        VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY=     (from 4a)
        VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY=    (from 4a)
        VAPID_SUBJECT=mailto:(the email push services may write to)
        R2_ENDPOINT=          (from 3b)
        R2_BUCKET=budget-h-backups
        R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID=     (from 3b)
        R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY= (from 3b)
      Watch for Notepad adding a byte order mark at the start (it did on
      his connection day): save as "UTF-8", not "UTF-8 with BOM".
   c. Send them to her app (this keeps them out of PowerShell's history):
        Get-Content .env.fly | fly secrets import --app budget-app-h-lbwr
   d. Delete .env.fly. Keep her BANK_TOKEN_KEY and R2 keys in the password
      manager, in entries of their own, named for her app. Without her
      BANK_TOKEN_KEY a rebuilt copy cannot open her bank connections.
   e. fly secrets list --app budget-app-h-lbwr shows the ten names (never
      the values).

5. THE FIRST DEPLOY (in her folder, on main)
        fly deploy --app budget-app-h-lbwr --ha=false --remote-only
   (--ha=false: one machine only; Fly would otherwise add a second.)
   Then:
        fly status --app budget-app-h-lbwr          one machine, started
        fly machine list --app budget-app-h-lbwr    exactly one
        fly logs --app budget-app-h-lbwr            "starting with empty books",
                                                    "Budget app: Plaid's test bank only, hosted",
                                                    "the test bank is connected"
   Open https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev/health: it says ok.
   In Cloudflare, budget-h-backups now has files under budget-h/.
   From the second deploy on, every deploy starts with the deploy check,
   in her folder (it reads her app's name from fly.toml):
        npm run deploy-check
   and deploys only on "The backup works.", then that exact image.

6. THE FIRST RESTORE TEST
        fly ssh console --app budget-app-h-lbwr -C "node /app/serve/restore-check.mjs"
   The last line must be: The backup works.
   Again once a month, and after anything odd.

7. THE UPTIME MONITORS (on his UptimeRobot account)
   a. New monitor: HTTP(s), https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev/health, every
      5 minutes, alert to his email (and hers, if she wants).
   b. New monitor: HTTP(s), https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev/health/backups,
      every 5 minutes, the same alerts.
   c. Name both for her app, so an alert says which app is down.

8. ON HER IPHONE (hers; he reads her the code)
   a. In Safari open https://budget-app-h-lbwr.fly.dev. Share > Add to Home
      Screen > Add. From now on open it from the home screen only.
   b. The login page: tap "Use a one-time code". On his PC, in her folder:
        fly ssh console --app budget-app-h-lbwr -C "node /app/serve/login-code.mjs"
      She types the 12 digits (good once, for 15 minutes). Face ID makes her
      passkey. Home opens.
   c. Setup day: Home says Start setup day. Go through it with the test
      bank's made-up accounts.
   d. Settings > Notifications > Turn on. Allow when iOS asks.
   e. Settings > Notifications > Send a test alert, with the phone locked to
      see it on the lock screen.
