PART A. WAITING FOR AN ANSWER

HA1 (plan Q2). A Wishlist item bought for less or more than its price.
    Rule 50.3 (approved text, PR 10).
    Option 1: mark it bought, and the bucket goes down by what it really
    cost. The difference stays in, or comes out of, Wishlist.
    Option 2: ask each time whether the difference comes from Wishlist or
    from wants.
    And a refunded item: (a) it stays bought, and she can add it again;
    (b) it comes back on the list in its old place.
    Recommended: option 1 and (a). It is what the bank did, it needs no
    extra question, and a refund often means the item was swapped, not
    dropped.
    Needed for PR 10.

HA2 (plan Q4). Her phone. Is it an iPhone?
    iPhone: notifications need the app added to the home screen (iOS 16.4
    or later), as on his. Android: Chrome works, with or without the home
    screen.
    Recommended: whatever she has. The app already works on both, and only
    the setup steps in docs/her-setup.txt differ. Testing stays in Chromium
    and WebKit, as now.
    Needed before her phone's setup (Phase 2).

HA3 (plan Q5). Her home time zone and her pay schedule.
    Both are settings already; only their starting values are needed. Time
    zone: where she lives (America/New_York to start, as upstream). Pay
    schedule: every 14 days, or twice a month (the 15th and the last day),
    read off her pay stubs.
    Recommended: set both on setup day from her own answers. Nothing has
    to be built for either.
    Needed before her setup day.

HA4 (plan Q6). Two paychecks waiting for her yes at once.
    Rule 51.2 (approved text, PR 4).
    Option 1: the older one is answered first, and a yes to the newer one
    is refused until then.
    Option 2: either order.
    Recommended: option 1. Pay periods have to start in date order (B53),
    and saying yes out of order would split the newer paycheck before the
    older one has closed its pay period.
    Needed for PR 4.

HA5 (plan Q7). Fly region for her app.
    ewr (New Jersey) as on his, or the region nearest where she lives.
    Recommended: ewr unless she is far from the East Coast. The app is one
    person's and light; a nearer region only saves a few tenths of a
    second per screen.
    Needed before his Fly steps (Phase 2).
