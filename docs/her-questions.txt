    waits for her yes: it keeps waiting for her yes. Only paychecks that
    post after the change are split by themselves. Turning it on changes
    nothing already split or waiting. Why: section 2 says what on and off
    do, not what happens to one already waiting, and splitting it without
    her yes would be a money change she did not make. Settings says so
    under the setting.

    longer be her paycheck (B54), holds no newer one up. While the setting
    is on, an early paycheck (3.7) is waiting for her yes too (51.3), so it
    is answered in order as well; and "This is my paycheck" on any other
    deposit (B52) dated after a waiting paycheck is refused the same way.
    With the setting off, the order is upstream's: any.
