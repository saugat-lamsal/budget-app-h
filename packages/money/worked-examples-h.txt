WORKED EXAMPLES, HER SETUP

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check the money math
of her setup by hand. Every amount is in dollars and cents. Upstream's
examples are in worked-examples.txt, unchanged.

Each example is also a test (packages/money/test/worked-examples-h.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" line of every example straight from this file
and compares it with what the app works out. If a number here is wrong, or
the app disagrees with it, the test fails.

Her setup has no mom reserve and no old card (rulebook, "Off in this
setup"). Unless an example says otherwise, the settings are her starting
ones: split 50% savings / 30% needs / 20% wants.

Rounding: each share is rounded DOWN to the cent. Any cents left over from
rounding go to savings (rule 3.3).


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 1. Her paycheck: nothing comes off first (3.3; 3.2 is off)
========================================================================

Before payday: every bucket is at $0.00.

Paycheck                                         2,345.67

Step 1. No mom reserve in this setup: nothing comes off the top.
        Rest to split                            2,345.67

Step 2. Split it.
        Savings 50% of 2,345.67 = 1,172.835  ->  1,172.83
        Needs   30% of 2,345.67 =   703.701  ->    703.70
        Wants   20% of 2,345.67 =   469.134  ->    469.13
        Rounded shares add to 2,345.66, so 0.01 is left over.
        The leftover 0.01 goes to savings: 1,172.83 + 0.01 = 1,172.84

Step 3. Add it back up: 1,172.84 + 703.70 + 469.13 = 2,345.67
        That is the paycheck exactly.

Check: savings 1,172.84 | needs 703.70 | wants 469.13 | mom reserve 0.00


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 2. A paycheck of one cent (3.3; 3.2 is off)
========================================================================

Before payday: every bucket is at $0.00.

Paycheck                                             0.01

Step 1. Split it.
        Savings 50% of 0.01 = 0.005  ->  0.00
        Needs   30% of 0.01 = 0.003  ->  0.00
        Wants   20% of 0.01 = 0.002  ->  0.00
        Rounded shares add to 0.00, so 0.01 is left over.
        The leftover 0.01 goes to savings.

Check: savings 0.01 | needs 0.00 | wants 0.00 | mom reserve 0.00


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 3. The app side of the self-check, with no mom reserve (12.2)
========================================================================

Setup day: savings $4,000.00, needs $1,000.00, wants $200.00, held for
others $0.00.

Then, both still waiting in review:
        Money in from a friend (a deposit)          60.00
        A purchase on the everyday card             12.50

App side = savings + needs + wants + held for others
           + unsorted money in - unsorted spending
         = 4,000.00 + 1,000.00 + 200.00 + 0.00 + 60.00 - 12.50
         = 5,247.50

Check: app side 5,247.50 | unsorted money in 60.00 | unsorted spending 12.50 | mom reserve 0.00
