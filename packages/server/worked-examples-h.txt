WORKED EXAMPLES, HER SETUP: THE SCREENS

Made-up numbers, worked out step by step, so you can check by hand what the
screens of her setup show. Every amount is in dollars and cents. Upstream's
examples are in worked-examples.txt, unchanged. The money math of her setup
is in packages/money/worked-examples-h.txt.

Each example is also a test (packages/server/test/worked-examples-h.test.ts).
The test reads the "Check:" line straight from this file and compares it
with what the server works out from the made-up bank.


========================================================================
EXAMPLE 1. A paycheck waits for her yes, on Home, then Payday
           (3.1, 51.1, 11.1, 12.1 to 12.3, 3.8)
========================================================================

Setup day, Oct 1. The made-up bank shows:

Checking                                         3,000.00
Savings                                          5,000.00
Everyday card, owed                                450.00

The starting money (14.2): 3,000.00 + 5,000.00 - 450.00 = 7,550.00
She assigns it: savings 4,000.00, wants 200.00, and needs the rest:
        7,550.00 - 4,000.00 - 200.00 = 3,350.00
Then she sets the paycheck rule. "Ask before splitting a paycheck" is on.

Oct 9: her paycheck of 2,500.00 posts to checking. It waits for her yes.

Home, while it waits:
        "Paycheck waiting for your yes", 2,500.00.
        Safe to spend (11.1) = wants - unsorted spending
                             = 200.00 - 0.00 = 200.00
        (The waiting paycheck is not part of it, 51.1.)
        App side (12.2) = 4,000.00 + 3,350.00 + 200.00 + 2,500.00 waiting
                        = 10,050.00
        Bank side (12.1) = checking 5,500.00 + savings 5,000.00
                           - card 450.00 = 10,050.00
        They match (12.3), so safe to spend is shown.

Oct 11: she says yes. The pay period starts Oct 9 (B53). The paycheck
splits 50/30/20:
        Savings 4,000.00 + 1,250.00 = 5,250.00
        Needs   3,350.00 +   750.00 = 4,100.00
        Wants     200.00 +   500.00 =   700.00
The leftover questions (3.4) wait in Review and move nothing yet.

Home: safe to spend = 700.00.
Payday (3.8): the savings account should hold the savings bucket,
5,250.00. It holds 5,000.00, so 250.00 is still to move.

Check: waiting on home 2,500.00 | safe to spend while waiting 200.00 | app side while waiting 10,050.00 | bank side while waiting 10,050.00 | savings after yes 5,250.00 | needs after yes 4,100.00 | wants after yes 700.00 | safe to spend after yes 700.00 | still to move 250.00
